About useful-indeed

A growing shelf of small, free web tools that run in your browser.

What this is

useful-indeed is a small collection of free web tools, each living on its own subdomain with its own design. Every tool does one job — PDFs, savings estimates, loan costs, random memes, raffles, a block-stacking game, choir lineups, code formatting, test data and focus timing — and does it inside your browser.

Why I made these

I don't trust most “free” tools online.

Most free websites that convert a PDF or crunch your numbers do it on their own servers. Your files and data leave your device, and you have to trust that they're deleted afterwards. Very few of them can honestly promise zero data retention.

I use these every day — so I made my own.

These are tools I actually need in my day-to-day work and life. Instead of uploading my stuff to a stranger's server every time, I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing to upload, nothing to retain.

Made for me, shared with you. Why not?

I built them for personal use, but there's no reason to keep them to myself. If they're useful to me, they might be useful to you too — free, no sign-up, and your data stays with you.

Frequently asked questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. There are no paywalls, trials or premium plans. The site may show ads to help cover costs, but every tool stays free to use.

Do I need an account?

No. None of the tools ask you to sign up or log in.

Where does my data go?

In almost every case, nowhere. The tools do their work with JavaScript inside your browser tab. Files you open are read on your device and are not uploaded. Each tool's privacy page lists the few exceptions, such as loading memes from a public meme service.

Will there be more tools?

Yes. The shelf grows whenever a small problem deserves a small tool.

Your privacy, in short

Each tool does its work in your browser. The main page does not store anything about you. Your browser may cache the page and its fonts so it loads faster next time. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Support the tools

Everything here is free, with no sign-up. If a tool saved you time, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi. It helps pay for hosting and new tools. Thank you!
