Why I made
this shelfa short letter

i.
I don’t trust “free” tools online.

Most free converters and calculators do the work on their own servers — your files and numbers leave your device, and you just have to hope they get deleted. Hardly any of them are truly zero data retention.

ii.
I use these every day, so I made my own.

PDFs, savings, loans, test data, focus time — these are things I need daily. So I built small versions that run entirely in the browser. Nothing gets uploaded, so there’s nothing to retain.

iii.
Made for me, shared with you — why not?

These started as tools for my own use. But if they’re useful to me, they might be useful to you too. Free, no sign-up, and your data stays yours.

— Sir Troymaker of useful-indeed
