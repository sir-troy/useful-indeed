Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In plain words: the tools on useful-indeed run in your browser. What you type or open is processed on your device and is not sent to us. We don't ask for accounts, and we don't sell data.

This Privacy Policy explains how useful-indeed (“we”, “us”), available at useful-indeed.com, handles information when you use it.

1. Information you enter

Each tool processes the files, text and numbers you provide with JavaScript inside your browser. This information is not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see, store or share it.

2. Information stored on your device

The main page does not store anything about you. Your browser may cache the page and its fonts so it loads faster next time.

3. Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the service secure and running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Third-party services

To work, the page loads some resources from these services. When it does, your browser connects to them directly and they may receive your IP address and browser details:

ServiceUsed for
Google Fontsto load the typefaces used on the page
Web3Formsto deliver messages sent through the Contact form

5. Advertising and cookies

To keep the tools free, this site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Ads are separate from the tool and never receive what you enter into it.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where the law requires it — for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland — you will be asked for consent before cookies for personalised ads are used. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

6. Contact form

If you send a message through the Contact page, the name, email address and message you type are sent through Web3Forms and delivered to our inbox. We use them only to reply to you and delete them when they are no longer needed. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

7. Analytics

We do not use analytics or tracking scripts of our own.

8. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices

You can clear anything stored on your device at any time through your browser settings. Because we don't collect personal information through the tools, there is nothing for us to delete — but if you have a request, contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? See the Contact page.
